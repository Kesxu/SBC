Our Story

Swaraj Book Club is a vibrant community of enthusiastic members and knowledge seekers. Founded with a passion for enhancing their skills, we bring together the opportunities to show your talent along with the skills which share a love for intellectual growth.

Our mission is to foster a corporate friendly behaviour among the members. So, they will be better prepared for the upcoming challenges and difficulties, we aim to create a space where ideas flourish and connections are made.
